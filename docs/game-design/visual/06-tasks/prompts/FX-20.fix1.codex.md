## What exists

`scraped-data/derived/fx-hitstar-codex/` holds the board-crop sheets. The review accepts as they are: the binary

## Why a fix

Opened at master and working size, on #061623 and on the board crop, in colour and gray:
- `layers()` puts BOTH the keyline and the cream edge INSIDE the star silhouette (distance <= 16 px from its border).
  The long rays are narrower than 32 px over most of their length, so they are drawn almost only in `mark.keyline`;
  the warm body is a decagon disc in the centre. On #061623 and on the dark board areas the long rays disappear, and
  at 36 px frames 2-5 read as a warm disc with a lumpy cream crown — a shape close to the dust discs.
- On dark ground the visible warm/cream area of frame 2 (the "full size" frame) is smaller than frame 1: the star
  seems to shrink at its peak.
- The short rays protrude only 0.12 R beyond the valleys (valley 0.43 R, short tip 0.55 R) and read as notches, not
  rays; the outer silhouette looks ragged.
- README recommends importing the mask «без mipmaps»: a 256 px cell drawn at 27-36 px without mips aliases and
  shimmers.

## Fix exactly this

1. **Layers: cream inside, keyline outside.** For every frame that has an outline, the body silhouette is the star
   itself; the cream edge `G` is the band inside the silhouette at distance <= 8 px from its border; the body `R` is
   the rest of the silhouette; the keyline `B` is the band OUTSIDE the silhouette at distance <= 8 px. Coverage stays
   binary and disjoint, A = max(R, G, B).
2. **Peak star geometry (frames 2-5).** 10 rays alternating long and short, tips 1 : 0.55. Long tip radius
   R_L = 94.4 px so the keyline's outer edge reaches 102.4 px (80 % of the cell by circumscribed diameter, as before).
   Valleys at 0.38 R_L, placed asymmetrically so the long rays are wider: the valleys of a long ray at +-22 deg from its
   axis, the short ray between them (its valleys at +-14 deg from its axis). One long ray points up. These numbers are
   a review prototype; you may adjust valley radius (0.36-0.42) and angles (+-20-24 deg) if a check below needs it, and
   write the final numbers in README.
3. **Frames 0-1.** Frame 0 stays body only (8 rays, outer radius 35 % of 102.4 px). Frame 1: 8 rays, body plus the
   outer keyline (no cream edge), keyline outer radius 70 % of 102.4 px — it then reads on the light board areas in
   gray. Frame 7 stays body only.
4. **Frame 6.** Same idea as now (rays retract toward the centre, the centre opens into a ring of base thickness
   20 px, opening r = 27 px), drawn with the layer rule of item 1; frame 7 stays a broken ring at 30 % of that ring
   thickness.
5. **New checks in `verify_hitstar.py` and `verification.json`** (keep every existing key; add these):
   - `body_reach_long_rays`: along the axis of each long ray in frame 2, the body `R` reaches >= 0.55 R_L (expected
     about 0.62); report the five values.
   - `visible_rays_on_navy`: the existing polar ray test, run on the visible shape on #061623 (R or G, keyline
     excluded) for frame 2 at 36 and 27 px nearest: expected 10 distinct rays at 36 px; at 27 px report the count
     honestly (>= 5 long rays required).
   - `visible_growth_on_navy`: visible (R or G) extent of frames 0, 1, 2 on #061623 (circumscribed radius and pixel
     area at master size): must grow 0 < 1 < 2.
   - `short_ray_prominence`: each short tip minus its adjacent valley radius, as a fraction of R_L (>= 0.15).
   - `exports`, `palette`, `gray`, `sizes`, `outside_folder`, `acceptance` in the format of 07 §1.2 (one acceptance
     object per clause of the task's `Acceptance:` line: `{passed, measured, expected, note}`).
6. **Sheets.** Rebuild every sheet from the scripts (colour and gray, navy and board crop, master, working 36 / 27 px
   nearest x4 and bilinear, timing strip, reduced-motion poster = frame 2). Add the new variant C to the A/B sheet as
   «C / recommended» (B becomes «B / first run»).
7. **README.md.** Update the geometry table and the fractions of R (ray count, R_L, short tip, valley radius and
   angles, edge widths, body reach, frame 6 and 7 numbers), the recommendation (C and why), and replace the import note:
   mipmaps ON (the flipbook plays at 27-36 px from a 256 px cell; the empty cell margin keeps neighbouring frames
   apart down to the 32 px mip), linear data / sRGB off, clamp; FX-21 confirms in UE. Keep the status «предложено» and
   keep the honest limitations (mask bleed, sub-pixel final ring, no engine check). Then rebuild
   `manifest-sha256.json` so it covers every file again, and the read-only `verify_hitstar.py` must pass.

Nothing else changes: no image generation, no new deliverables, no files outside the two package folders, no git,
