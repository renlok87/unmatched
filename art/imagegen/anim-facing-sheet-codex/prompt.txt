Task AN-26 — Facing sheet: idle three-quarter, turn to the target, return. Trigger: the idle facing is recomputed when a snapshot is applied, when a move ends and when an attack ends; the attack turn is the combat stage event face, 120 ms before the lunge. Timing: idle yaw = the direction from the figure to the camera of this client in the ground plane, shifted toward the nearest living enemy by at most 45 deg; dead band 10 deg; a changed idle yaw is reached in 150 ms along the shortest arc; the attack turn takes 120 ms and ends exactly at the start of the lunge, inside the 300 ms score pause after the slam; the attack yaw points at the target but never more than 90 deg off the camera axis; LungeAttack 583 ms (14 frames at 24 fps), contact frame 7 = 292 ms for King Arthur and the Harpy, frame 8 = 333 ms for Merlin and Medusa; return to the idle yaw 150 ms after the clip ends; the target does not turn; turn and return are not scaled by the animation speed (the lunge is: play rate 2 / 1 / 0.67 at x0.5 / x1 / x1.5); reduced motion and speed none make the turn and the return instant. Keyframes: -300 ms: score pause starts, attacker at its idle yaw; -120 ms: turn to the target starts; 0 ms: facing the target, LungeAttack starts; 292 ms: contact (King Arthur, Harpy); 333 ms: contact (Merlin, Medusa); 583 ms: clip ends, return starts; 733 ms: idle yaw restored.
