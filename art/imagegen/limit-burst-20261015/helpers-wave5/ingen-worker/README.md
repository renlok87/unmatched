## Self-review and actual limitations

Attempt 01: one complete adult figure and a complete two-disc dark stone base; front-facing head and body; both arms gently bent away from the torso; readable charcoal gaps around arms and between legs; right-hand tool on viewer left pointing down and away; left hand empty and visible. No obvious fused extra hand or missing limb was observed. Some gripping fingers are naturally occluded by the tool; this is visual self-review, not an anatomical certification. Workwear and material detail are clear. No visible text, logo, UI, dinosaurs, scenery or VFX.

**Actual framing failure:** hair begins at approximately y=70 of 1536 (4.6%), rather than the requested 6% (y≈92). Pedestal bottom is approximately y=1369 (89.1%), rather than exactly 90% (y≈1382). These are visual landmark estimates, not segmentation measurements. The base is nearly edge-on, with a very thin bright upper rim; strict zero-visible-top geometry cannot be certified from this raster. Face and torso read front-on, but camera projection cannot be proved mathematically from a generated image. The charcoal backdrop has a soft studio gradient rather than a perfectly constant pixel value.

Attempt 02: the permitted framing correction preserved the overall technician design and hand assignment but moved the pedestal too high. Hair starts approximately y=107 (7.0%) and the base ends approximately y=1308 (85.2%). It did not achieve the exact requested framing and is not selected. Attempt 01 is closer overall to the requested composition. No more correction calls were made.

The selected image remains **proposed with deviations** for independent review. Originality and absence of resemblance are design intent, not legal or biometric certification. No back/side views, 3D geometry, rig, animation, materials, import, performance or game integration have been produced or tested.
